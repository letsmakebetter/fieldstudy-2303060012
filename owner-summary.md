# Website Audit – Owner Summary

**Website:** Merit Hotels  
**Audit Date:** 5 November 2026

## Overall Assessment

Merit Hotels provides a secure website where visitors can browse hotels and begin a reservation. However, the audit identified problems with reservation consistency, accessibility and loading performance. These issues may cause confusion and make the booking experience less reliable.

## Three Main Priorities

### 1. Fix the Reservation Process – High Priority

Visitors' selected hotel, dates and guest information are not consistently preserved. After searching, returning to the homepage, using browser navigation, refreshing the page or experiencing a temporary internet interruption, some selections may disappear or reset. The homepage may also switch from Turkish to English. In some cases, the displayed guest options do not match the selected hotel.

**Recommendation:** Preserve reservation details and the selected language throughout the entire booking journey. Ensure that the selected hotel and displayed guest options always match.

### 2. Improve Website Accessibility – High Priority

Lighthouse gave the homepage an accessibility score of 75/100 and the inner hotel page 77/100. Problems included missing image alternative text, buttons and links without accessible names, insufficient colour contrast and form label issues.

**Recommendation:** Improve screen-reader support, keyboard accessibility, image descriptions, form labels and text contrast.

### 3. Improve Loading Performance – Medium Priority

The homepage transferred approximately 11.9 MB of data during the measured Network test. Visitors with slower internet connections may experience longer loading times.

**Recommendation:** Optimise images, compress large resources and reduce unnecessary network requests.

## Additional Improvements

- Make footer phone numbers and email addresses clickable.
- Improve gallery touchpad navigation and make photo indicators easier to see.
- Display clear warnings for unsuitable room and guest combinations.
- Use the correct Merit Hotels favicon consistently across Turkish pages.
- Preserve the selected language when returning to the homepage.
- Improve unclear labels such as "Details", "Continue" and "Search".
- Make the agency discount code control more accessible to keyboard users.

## Conclusion

The most urgent improvement is to make the reservation process reliable and consistent. Visitors should not lose their selected information or language when moving between pages. Improving this process, together with accessibility and performance, would help Merit Hotels provide a better experience and reduce visitor frustration.
